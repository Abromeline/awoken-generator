Support — the Awokin

Last updated: October 19, 2026

The Awokin is a small, independently made game, tended by its creator. Most troubles are answered below. Anything else, ask in the Awokin Discord server — the same server the Table is played in — and the Keeper will answer there.

I forgot my password

If your Tender has an email on file, use “Forgot your password?” on the sign-in page: a reset link is mailed to you. It lives for one hour and works once, and the moment it lands, your old password and every session it ever made are ended — you arrive signed in on the device in your hand.

No email on file, or the inbox is gone? The Keeper still resets by hand: ask in the Awokin Discord server, say your Tender name, and a new password will be set for you. To plant an email for next time, add one in the naming panel when you are signed in — it is used for password recovery and nothing else.

The Table will not open, or will not seat me

  • The Table lives inside Discord as an Activity. Open it from the server's Activities, in a channel where Activities are allowed.
  • To play with your real deck you need a binding code: in the game, open the Table panel and choose “Show my binding code”, then spend the code in the bind strip above your hand at the Table. A code fades in fifteen minutes and works once — if one fails, simply mint a fresh one.
  • The Table seats only Tenders who have chosen a class (from level 3, on your character sheet). Classless Tenders are turned away at the door until they choose.
  • Stepped away mid-game? Your seat is held for ten minutes; come back and it is yours again.

Purchases and wake credits

  • Wake packs are sold through Stripe; the game never sees your card. Credits are granted when the payment completes — if a pack does not arrive within a few minutes, keep your Stripe receipt and ask in the Discord.
  • Bought the wrong thing, or a payment was taken twice? Ask in the Discord with your receipt and it will be made right, by refund or by credits.

Something in the game looks wrong

  • A missing Awokin, a stuck march, a number that cannot be right: note what you were doing and roughly when, and tell us in the Discord. Game state is kept on the server, so most tangles can be unpicked from the record.
  • There is also a small feedback note inside the game itself, on your Tender's page.
  • A hard refresh (reload the page) cures most display ghosts after an update.

Your account and your data

You may ask what the game holds about you, or ask for your account and its data to be deleted, at any time — ask in the Discord server and it will be done. What the game holds, and what never happens to it, is set out in the Privacy Policy.
