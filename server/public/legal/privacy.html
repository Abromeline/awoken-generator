Privacy Policy — the Awoken

Last updated: October 16, 2026

The Awoken is a small, independently made game: a creature-collection and territory game on the web, together with the Table, a Discord Activity where players gather to play its card game together. The game is operated by its creator ("we", "the Operator"). This policy says plainly what information the game holds, why, and what never happens to it.

What the game holds

  • Your Tender account. The name you choose, a hashed password (never the password itself), and a sign-in token your browser keeps so you stay signed in.
  • Your game. Your Awoken, decks, territories, energy, stories you write to your Awoken, and similar gameplay state. This is the game itself; it cannot be played without it.
  • At the Table (Discord Activity). When you open the Table inside Discord and sign in, Discord tells us your Discord user ID and display name so the Table can name you and seat you. While you play, the Table holds what the game needs in the open: your name, how many cards you hold, your rolls (written into the session's Tale), and — privately, sent only to you — your hand, your personal notes, and the packing list you type.
  • Stories you choose to send. Stories are private by default. Only if you press "Send to Discord" on a story does it travel: your Tender name, the Awoken's name, the story text, and the Awoken's card image are posted into the project's Discord channel. Sending is always your choice, story by story.
  • Purchases. Payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your card number. We keep a record of completed purchases (what was bought, when, and the wake credits granted) so your account can be credited and purchase questions answered.
  • Ordinary server records. Like any web service, the host keeps short-lived technical logs (such as IP address and request times) needed to run and protect the service.

What we never do

  • We never sell personal information, to anyone, ever.
  • We run no advertising and no advertising or cross-site trackers.
  • We never share your information with data brokers or marketers.
  • Your private game data — your stories, notes, and hand at the Table — is never shown to other players. At the Table, other players' hands are not merely hidden; they are never sent.

Who else touches the data

  • Discord — only what the Activity needs to seat you (your Discord identity), what you choose to send there (stories), and whatever you do inside Discord itself, which is governed by Discord's own privacy policy.
  • Stripe — to process purchases you choose to make.
  • Railway — the hosting provider the game runs on; it stores the game's database as our infrastructure.

No one else receives your information, except if the law ever required it.

Your choices

  • You may ask for your account and its data to be deleted, or ask what we hold about you, at any time — contact us through the project's Discord server. Deletion removes your Tender, your Awoken, and your Table notes and packing.
  • A story you sent to Discord lives in that Discord channel afterwards; ask there or ask us and we will remove it where we can.
  • Leaving the Table or unbinding removes your seat; your notes and packing are removed on request.

Children

The Awoken is not directed at children under 13, and Discord itself requires its users to be 13 or older. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date before it takes effect.

Contact

Reach the Operator through the Awoken project's Discord server — the same server the Table is played in.
